Act as an expert startup pitch coach and scriptwriter. My team of 4 members is presenting our hackathon project to teachers and judges. Our project is called **DSLC (Digital Solutions for Local Communities)**. We need a simple, easy-to-understand 10-slide presentation script. 

**Here is the context about our project:**
*   **The Problem:** In developing tier-2 cities (like Ranchi, Jharkhand), citizens face massive friction. It's incredibly hard to find verified local professionals (plumbers, electricians). People don't know which local shops are open. Worst of all, reporting civic issues (potholes, broken streetlights, water leaks) to the local municipal corporation (RMC) is a broken, paper-based, and ignored process.
*   **The Solution:** DSLC is a unified, one-stop digital platform for the entire city. 
    1. **Local Services:** Book background-checked professionals instantly.
    2. **Local Businesses:** Discover and navigate to nearby shops.
    3. **Community Pulse:** Report civic issues with photos, track them on a live map, and route them directly to the RMC.
    4. **Emergency Hub:** One-tap SOS buttons for police, fire, and hospitals.
    5. **DSLC AI (Powered by Google Gemini):** A built-in AI assistant where users can just type "there is a water leak outside my house" and the AI instantly categorizes it and guides them to the right solution.

**What I need from you:**
Create a 10-slide pitch deck structure. For each slide, please provide:
1.  **Slide Title**
2.  **Visual / Bullet Points** (What goes on the screen, keep it very minimal)
3.  **Speaker Script** (Exactly what the team member should say)

**Constraints:**
*   There are 4 team members (Speaker 1, Speaker 2, Speaker 3, Speaker 4). Divide the speaking parts evenly so everyone gets a chance to shine.
*   Keep the script conversational, confident, and simple enough that teachers and non-technical judges will immediately understand why this is a winning idea. Do not use overly complex technical jargon.
*   Make sure the transition between speakers feels natural.
